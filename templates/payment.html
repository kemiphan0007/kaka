Why Bitcoin?

International SWIFT bank transfers typically take 1–3 days, while credit card (CC) payments may take up to one week to process. In contrast, Bitcoin payments can typically be received within approximately 30 minutes.

This faster payment process allows us to confirm your order sooner, speed up order processing, and arrange shipment on the same day, helping you receive your products sooner.

Bitcoin also allows us to receive and verify payments directly, without relying on traditional bank transfer processing times. Our Bitcoin payment process has been part of our standard payment procedure since 2021, helping us maintain a faster and more consistent order-processing workflow.

How It Works

STEP 01 Send BTC Send the BTC equivalent of your USD invoice to the payment address provided above.
STEP 02 Payment Confirmation Upload your transaction proof. We verify the transaction and confirm receipt of payment.
STEP 03 Order Processing Once payment is confirmed, we proceed with order verification and shipment preparation.

Important

Before sending your payment, carefully verify the payment address and network. Cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible once confirmed on the network.

We cannot be responsible for funds sent to an incorrect address or through an unsupported network. Please ensure that the BTC amount sent matches the USD invoice equivalent. Any shortfall may require an additional payment.
